  • The launcher adds OpenRouter routing preferences (one provider, no fallbacks) and repeats the reasoning effort in OpenRouter's native reasoning field; messages, tools and generation settings are unchanged.
  • Sampling defaults other than temperature (top_p, top_k) are the provider's, which may differ from the paper's DeepSeek API.
  • A launcher enforces a spending cap before requests.
  • Context-overflow episodes are treated as invalid rather than scored.
  • Sampling is not seeded, so reruns are not token-identical.
  • 10 trajectories instead of 50.
  • OpenRouter usage omitted cache read/write fields; prompt tokens are charged conservatively as uncached.
